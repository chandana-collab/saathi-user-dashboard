How each step is counted
Source
Every tab of the Saathi conversation data log shared in the "Saathi Data logs - Monthly" thread, one row per message. Tabs overlap, so a message that appears in more than one tab is counted once. New tabs are picked up automatically at each sync.
District
Matched to Bihar's 38 districts (plus a few outside Bihar) from what users typed, in Hindi or English, allowing small spelling mistakes. Names that match no district are listed as "Unrecognised".
Unique user
A distinct user_id with at least one message in the month.
Started onboarding
mapping_status is "Profile found". "Profile not found" users are counted as dropping off at login.
Completed onboarding
Profile found, with role and district captured.
Started a conversation
Sent at least one message that was not an answer to Saathi's onboarding questions (name, role, school, state, district).
Received an answer or MIP
Saathi sent a substantive reply: one drawing on the knowledge base, a reply of 400+ characters, or an improvement plan.
MIP steps
Detected from Saathi's own wording: "Let me understand… build a plan" (challenge), "here is your Goal", "Objective", "Here is your Improvement Plan", and "ready to download" in a conversation that produced a plan.
Not removed
Internal and test accounts (for example a user named "Testing") are still counted.
